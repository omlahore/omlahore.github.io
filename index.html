Project 01Swift · SwiftUI · XPCmacOS 27

          
RemoveMacAI

        

        

          
          

            
A Mac app and command-line tool that turns off Apple Intelligence, deletes its on-device models and applies 40 privacy and system tweaks. System Integrity Protection stays on, and you can revert every change.
